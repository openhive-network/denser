# Locale review of #1727

The terms the #1698 review left open, as decided on the issue (2026-11-20) after comparing hive.blog,
the Hive wallet, Ecency and PeakD: ru witness свидетель; zh delegation 委托 (代理 only for the
witness-voting proxy), payout 收益 (earnings) and 结算 (the payout moment), curation rewards 策展奖励;
fr paiement (the payout event), gains (amounts earned), récompenses de curation; it keeps "witness"
(no string changed). Each line is `<set> <key>: "old" → "new"`, for a native speaker to check the
batch; the terms are still machine-chosen and may be refined.

Besides these, the blog's and the wallet's unused `login_form.password_hbauth_placeholder`,
`title_hbauth_form` and `use_hbauth` keys ("Hbauth") are gone from every locale: the dialogs show
the smart-signer copies.
